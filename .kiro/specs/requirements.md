# Eco Agri Lama: Dark Harvest Edition

## System Overview
Eco Agri Lama is an AI-powered agricultural companion for smallholder farmers, offering organic remedy guides, soil health tips, and crop management advice. The app features a mobile-first Halloween-themed UI with spooky dark visuals and audio feedback.

## Requirements & Scope
- **Interactive Advice Chat**: Provide real-time agricultural remedies and organic pest control advice.
- **Dark Harvest UI**: Custom dark mode featuring Halloween-inspired graphics, spooky sound toggles, and high-contrast accessibility.
- **Offline & Telemetry Support**: Local state management and fallback mechanisms for remote field use.
