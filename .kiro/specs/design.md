# Design Specification: Eco Agri Lama

## Visual Architecture
- **Theme**: Dark Harvest Halloween edition.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Background: Dark Violet `#0a0512`
  - Accents: Eerie Green `#39ff14` & Pumpkin Orange `#ff7518`
  - Text: High-contrast Off-White `#e2e8f0`

## Component Structure
- `Chat.jsx`: Core conversational engine and advisory dashboard.
- `SoundToggle`: Audio controller for Creak and Potion sound effects.
- `FieldTelemetry`: Local storage syncer for offline farm data.
